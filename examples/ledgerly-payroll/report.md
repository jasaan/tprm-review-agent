## Summary

Ledgerly Payroll will replace in-house payroll spreadsheets for 180 employees. It will receive names, addresses, SSNs, salaries and bank account numbers through a one-way HR sync, and 6 staff will sign in through SSO. The vendor is provisionally Tier 2 (High) because of employee PII and financial data, an HR-system integration and an important process with a one-cycle manual workaround. Open intake questions could move it to Tier 1: funding-debit or tax-agent authority, whether the workaround survives migration, HR connector credentials and fields, and record volume. The SOC 2 Type II report (July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026) is an excerpt with selected tests only. It has an unqualified opinion, covers Ledgerly Payroll and shows no exceptions for workforce MFA, access reviews, pen testing, logging, incident response, backups and change management. The DPA excerpt includes subprocessor notice, 72-hour breach notice, deletion and no ML training, but it is unsigned, so the contract-based domains (2, 8, 10, 12) are partially met. No claim was contradicted by evidence. The 1-hour RPO claim is unsupported, since only daily backups and restore tests are evidenced. Three medium findings remain: encryption of SSNs and bank data rests on claims only, oversight of the carved-out AWS and Evergreen Bank is not evidenced, and customer-side authentication is not evidenced (portal MFA, bank-detail change verification, SSO enforcement), along with vendor-staff deprovisioning. Several low evidence gaps remain. The recommendation is approve with conditions, subject to the requesting team's intake answers.

### F-01: Encryption of SSNs and bank account data not independently evidenced (medium)

- **Domain:** 4. Data protection and encryption
- **What's wrong:** Encryption in transit, encryption at rest and field-level encryption of bank account numbers are supported only by questionnaire answers. The vendor will hold SSNs and bank account numbers for all employees, so this claim is important for a Tier 2 vendor. Confidentiality is in SOC 2 scope, so the full report may contain relevant tests. Because the full report and DPA Annex 2 are low-cost to obtain, resolving this before employee data is loaded is advisable.
- **Evidence:** questionnaire.md DAT-1: 'TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256 at rest with AWS KMS; bank account numbers additionally encrypted at the field level.' The SOC 2 excerpt ('Tests of Controls and Results (selected)') contains no encryption test. DPA Section 7 refers to 'Annex 2', which was not provided. (sources: questionnaire.md, soc2-type2-excerpt.md, dpa-excerpt.md)
- **Recommendation:** Request DPA Annex 2 and the full SOC 2 report, including the Section 4 tests covering encryption in transit and at rest and key management (CC6.1/CC6.7). Also request evidence of field-level encryption of bank account numbers and of how SSNs are protected. Close the finding if tested without exception. Preferably resolve before data is loaded.

### F-02: Oversight of carved-out subservice organizations handling payroll data not evidenced (medium)

- **Domain:** 2. Supply chain and subprocessors
- **What's wrong:** AWS (hosting) and Evergreen Bank (ACH origination using employee bank account numbers) are carved out of the SOC 2 report, so the auditor did not test their controls. No evidence shows that Ledgerly reviews their assurance reports or flows down security terms to them.
- **Evidence:** soc2-type2-excerpt.md Section 3 lists both AWS and Evergreen Bank with Method 'Carved out'. No vendor-management control appears in the selected Section 4 tests. (sources: soc2-type2-excerpt.md, questionnaire.md)
- **Recommendation:** Request evidence of Ledgerly's annual review of the AWS and Evergreen Bank assurance reports (SOC report reviews and CUEC mapping), or the full SOC 2 test of vendor management (CC9.2). Also request confirmation that security and breach-notification terms flow down to Evergreen Bank.

### F-09: Customer-side authentication, portal account protection and vendor-staff deprovisioning not evidenced (medium)

- **Domain:** 3. Identity and access management
- **What's wrong:** The SOC 2 evidence covers only Ledgerly's own workforce MFA and quarterly access reviews. Several controls that protect our accounts are not evidenced: customer SAML SSO/SCIM and enforcement of our IdP's MFA (claim only); MFA for employee self-service portal accounts; out-of-band verification and notification of direct-deposit bank-detail changes (payroll-diversion risk); and timely deprovisioning and least privilege for Ledgerly staff. Whether the portal will be enabled for all 180 employees is an open intake question.
- **Evidence:** soc2-type2-excerpt.md AC-02 (CC6.1): 'MFA enforced for all workforce and administrative access'. AC-05 (CC6.2): quarterly access reviews. Section 3 scope includes 'employee self-service portal'. questionnaire.md IAM-2: 'Yes, SAML 2.0 and SCIM provisioning' (claim). No document addresses end-user MFA or verification of bank-detail changes. (sources: soc2-type2-excerpt.md, questionnaire.md, intake.md)
- **Recommendation:** Request evidence of end-user portal MFA and of out-of-band verification and dual notification of bank-detail changes, plus the full SOC 2 tests of joiner/mover/leaver and least-privilege controls (CC6.2/CC6.3) and confirmation that customer SAML SSO enforces our IdP's MFA. Re-rate to high if the portal is enabled for employees and these controls cannot be evidenced. Do not enable employee portal self-service changes to bank details until resolved.

### F-06: 1-hour RPO and RTO claims unsupported; only daily backups and restore tests evidenced (low)

- **Domain:** 9. Business continuity and backup
- **What's wrong:** Daily backups and quarterly restore tests are evidenced. The questionnaire's '1 hour' RPO 'tested quarterly' is in tension with this evidence. Daily backups alone cannot deliver a 1-hour RPO, and no replication or point-in-time recovery mechanism is evidenced. A restore test is also not a test of achieved RTO/RPO. The claim is classified as unsupported rather than contradicted, because another recovery mechanism may exist. If the vendor confirms that daily backups are the only mechanism, this becomes a contradiction and a high finding. Payroll timing and tax deadlines are important to the business, and no BC/DR plan or exercise was provided.
- **Evidence:** soc2-type2-excerpt.md BC-02 (A1.2): 'Daily backups; quarterly restore tests', no exceptions. questionnaire.md BCP-1: 'RTO 8 hours, RPO 1 hour; tested quarterly' (claim). (sources: soc2-type2-excerpt.md, questionnaire.md)
- **Recommendation:** Ask the vendor to identify the mechanism supporting the 1-hour RPO and to provide the most recent DR exercise showing achieved RTO and RPO. Also request the BC plan's provisions for meeting payroll and tax-filing deadlines during an outage. Re-rate to high and record a contradiction if daily backups are the only mechanism.

### F-07: US data location and US-based support not contractually committed (low)

- **Domain:** 10. Privacy and data handling
- **What's wrong:** US-only storage and processing is consistent with the SOC 2 AWS regions, but the DPA excerpt contains no data-location commitment. US-based support staff is a claim only. Data may also be retained for about 4 years after termination, and it is unclear whether bank account numbers fall within that retention.
- **Evidence:** questionnaire.md DAT-2: 'United States only (AWS us-east-1 and us-east-2). Support staff are US-based.' soc2-type2-excerpt.md Section 3 lists AWS us-east-1/us-east-2. dpa-excerpt.md has no location clause. questionnaire.md PRV-2 gives retention of 'generally 4 years under IRS rules'. (sources: questionnaire.md, soc2-type2-excerpt.md, dpa-excerpt.md)
- **Recommendation:** Seek a DPA clause restricting storage, processing and support access to the United States. Ask the vendor to confirm which data elements are kept during the legal retention period, and that data not required by law (for example bank account numbers) is deleted at the standard 30-day point.

### F-08: No-ML-training commitment limited to personal data and not yet signed (low)

- **Domain:** 12. AI and automated processing
- **What's wrong:** The DPA's ML prohibition covers 'Customer Personal Data' only, while the questionnaire states more broadly that 'customer data' is not used for model training. Non-personal customer data, such as aggregate payroll or company financial data, is not covered. The DPA is also not yet executed.
- **Evidence:** dpa-excerpt.md Section 12: 'will not use Customer Personal Data to train or improve machine learning models.' questionnaire.md PRV-1: 'Customer data is not used for model training.' intake.md: 'Data Processing Addendum under review'. (sources: dpa-excerpt.md, questionnaire.md, intake.md)
- **Recommendation:** Request that DPA Section 12 (or the order form) be extended to all Customer Data, including aggregated or de-identified derivatives, or that the vendor confirm in writing how non-personal data is used. Execute the DPA before signature.
